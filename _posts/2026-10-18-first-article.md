If you teach, you have probably heard about the 7 Modules and their growing importance in the teacher licensing process. Unfortunately, navigating these modules can be frustrating, especially when information is unclear or conflicting advice leaves educators uncertain about the requirements. KruCrew67 brings firsthand insights from educators who have completed the modules and obtained their P-Licenses, making practical guidance easier to access. Through upcoming posts, we will share module-specific advice and licensing updates, helping you navigate the process while staying focused on teaching.
