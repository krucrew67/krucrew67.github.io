**The Goal**

KruCrew67 is a community-driven space dedicated to helping education professionals navigate the 7 Modules and teacher licensing requirements with greater clarity and confidence. We welcome educators at every stage of their careers, from newcomers entering the profession to experienced teachers pursuing their professional qualifications. Our goal is to create a supportive network where members can connect and learn from one another. 

**How We Chose the Name**

The name KruCrew67 combines Thai teaching culture, a spirit of teamwork, and a playful reference to the popular “67” meme. In Thailand, “Kru” (ครู) means teacher, while “Crew” reflects the value of working together towards a common goal. The number “67” carries several layers of meaning. The number “67” also marks the journey’s beginning, as initial module registration took place in 2024 (2567 in the Thai Buddhist calendar). This connection continues through to Modules 6 and 7, which represent the final stages of the course while also providing a playful nod to the familiar “67” meme. Much like the meme’s up-and-down hand movement, the path to professional licensing can have its unexpected twists and turns. From the very beginning to the final steps, the number “67” has remained a recurring theme, symbolising a shared journey of unity, resilience, and a little humour.

**The Problem We Currently See**

Navigating the teacher licensing process can be challenging when information is inconsistent, requirements change, or advice is interpreted differently. Although guidance is available online, it often lacks the practical detail needed to address real situations and specific concerns. Having completed all 7 Modules and obtained our P-Licenses, we have experienced these difficulties firsthand and recognise the need for more reliable, accessible information.

**The Solution We Aim to Provide**

Through KruCrew67, we aim to bridge these information gaps by offering straightforward explanations, firsthand experiences, and relevant updates that educators can put into practice. Our members include professionals who have successfully earned their P-Licenses, as well as those who have undertaken the B-License examination. By drawing on this collective experience, we hope to help others make informed decisions, avoid unnecessary setbacks, and spend less time worrying about licensing so they can focus on what matters most: teaching.
