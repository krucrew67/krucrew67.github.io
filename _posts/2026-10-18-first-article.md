**The Goal**

This is community-driven space professionals within the educational sector can find clear and practical guidance on the 7 Modules and teacher licensing requirements. KruCrew67 aims to bring professionals together to learn from those who have successfully or currently completing the this process. We welcome everyone from newcomers beginning their teaching careers to experiencing professionals working towards their professional licenses. 

**How We Chose the Name**

The name Kru Crew 67 brings together Thai teaching culture, a sense of community, and a playful reference to the popular “67” meme. In Thailand, “Kru” (ครู) means teacher, while “Crew” represents a group of educators supporting one another through shared experiences and challenges. The number “67” connects both to the familiar classroom meme and to Modules 6 and 7, the final modules of the course. Much like the meme’s up-and-down hand movement, the licensing journey can feel unpredictable, with changing requirements and sometimes conflicting advice along the way. Together, Kru Crew 67 represents a community of professionals helping one another navigate these uncertainties with greater clarity, confidence, and a little humour

**The Problem We Currently See**

Many professionals in struggle to navigate the teacher licensing process because information can be inconsistent. Even when guidance is available online, it does not always address the practical questions and challenges educators encounter throughout their journey. Having completed the 7 Modules and obtained our P-Licenses, we understand these frustrations firsthand, including the uncertainty surrounding changing requirements and different interpretations.

**The Solution We Aim to Provide**

Through KruCrew67, we aim to simplify the licensing journey by sharing practical advice, personal experiences, and relevant updates that educators can confidently use. Our community brings together educators who have completed the 7 Modules, obtained their P-Licenses, and, in some cases, undertaken the B-License examination. By making information easier to understand and access, we hope to reduce unnecessary stress and help educators focus on what matters most: teaching.
