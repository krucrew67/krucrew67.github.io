---

If you are an educator working in the Kingdom of Thailand, chances are you have heard about the “7 Modules” at some point. Whether you teach in a government school or an international school, Thailand’s evolving teacher licensing requirements are becoming an increasingly important part of the profession. For many educators, understanding these requirements and knowing how to navigate the process can feel overwhelming, particularly when clear and reliable guidance is difficult to find. Perhaps you have already discussed the modules with colleagues or your school's administration. You may have spent hours researching the requirements online, only to find conflicting information or unanswered questions. Alternatively, you might be hearing about the 7 Modules for the very first time. Regardless of where you are in the process, having access to practical advice from educators who have been through it can make all the difference.

That is exactly why this blog was created.
